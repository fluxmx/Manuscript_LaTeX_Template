Begin with the broad scientific question and explain why it matters. Identify the
specific gap that motivates the study, then state what was done and what was
found. Give the central result in concrete terms, including its magnitude and
uncertainty where appropriate. End by explaining how the finding changes the
understanding of the problem. This paragraph is a writing guide rather than a
scientific claim; replace it with a self-contained summary of your own work.
Avoid undefined abbreviations, technical digressions and claims that go beyond
the evidence presented in the article.
